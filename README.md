## About me

I'm Florent, a developer based in Belgium and the person behind
**[Avenue du Web](https://avenueduweb.be)**.

My projects range from web applications and business tools to software,
automation and hardware-connected systems. I like understanding what happens
below the abstraction layer just as much as building the product above it.

> **Use the simplest technology that properly solves the problem.**

No unnecessary layers. No framework just because it's fashionable.
Build what is useful, understand what it does, and keep it maintainable.

## 42, Overflow & what came next

**42 Brussels** gave me a strong foundation in low-level programming,
algorithms, Unix systems, networking and software architecture.

`Overflow` was my nickname there — partly because my name is **Flo**,
mostly because I tend to push projects further than initially planned.

The occasional `int`, buffer or memory overflow probably didn't help either.

Today, I apply that foundation to real-world projects through **Avenue du Web**.
